Platform: VC Connect • Version 1.0 | Effective Date: November 2026

1. Clinical Portal Purpose & Doctor Responsibility

The VC Connect platform by VelourCare Ortho is designed exclusively for licensed orthodontists, dentists, digital planning specialists, and clear aligner fabrication laboratories. The treating clinician maintains sole legal and professional responsibility for patient examination, diagnosis, treatment plan approval, and clinical oversight.

2. Treatment Plan Approval & Manufacturing Authorization

Aligner production will only commence upon explicit digital approval of the treatment plan by the treating doctor or designated clinical lead. Approving a treatment plan authorizes VelourCare to proceed with 3D model printing, thermoforming, laser trimming, and laser tooth numbering.

3. Confidentiality & Account Security

Each authorized portal user must maintain a distinct, individual account with multi-character password complexity and optional two-factor authentication. Account sharing or transmitting clinical credentials is strictly prohibited. Users must immediately notify VelourCare of any suspected credential compromise.

4. Invoicing and Payments

Invoices are generated upon treatment plan approval or manufacturing initiation. Once issued or marked paid, invoices are locked to maintain audit and tax integrity. Any adjustments or cancellations are processed via formal credit notes.

5. Termination & Access Revocation

VelourCare reserves the right to suspend or revoke portal access immediately upon notice of clinical license suspension, violation of patient data security, or breach of commercial terms.
