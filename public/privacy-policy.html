Privacy Policy

Platform: VC Connect • Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective Date: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

VC Connect by VelourCare Ortho collects and processes information required exclusively for orthodontic diagnosis, digital treatment staging, 3D printing of clear aligners, and clinical invoicing:

  • Patient Protected Health Information (PHI): Patient full name, age, gender, intraoral photographs, 3D digital impressions (STL, PLY, OBJ, O3C), panoramic and cephalometric radiographs, clinical prescriptions, and doctor instructions.
  • Clinician & Clinic Details: Doctor name, clinic business address, email, telephone, license details, and billing identifiers.
  • System & Access Telemetry: Log timestamps, IP addresses, browser user-agent, session identifiers, and audit logs of all clinical actions.

2. How We Use and Protect Health Data

Patient health information is processed strictly to deliver custom orthodontic aligners, generate staging simulations, and facilitate laboratory manufacturing. We implement rigorous technical and administrative safeguards:

  • Zero Public Scans: Medical 3D files and scans are encrypted and accessible only through authenticated, scoped, short-lived signed sessions.
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation: Strict data-level separation guarantees that Clinic A can never access or view patients, scans, or invoices belonging to Clinic B.
  • Data Minimization: Only data directly required for treatment planning and manufacturing is requested.

3. Role-Based Access Controls

Access within VC Connect is governed by the Principle of Least Privilege:

  • Treating Doctor / Clinic: Full access to own patient cases, own invoices, and treatment plan approval.
  • CAD Planner: Access to digital files and clinical photos to produce 3D aligner staging; blocked from financial data.
  • Production Lab: Access restricted to manufacturing queue files (STL step models); blocked from patient financial records.
  • Billing / Accounts: Access to invoices and payment tracking; blocked from clinical patient records.

4. Data Retention and Erasure

Clinical patient records are maintained in compliance with UAE Federal Health Data Law No. 2 of 2019 and international medical records retention mandates. In accordance with patient rights, data can be requested for export or soft-archived upon verified clinical authorization.

5. Contact Data Protection Officer

For data inquiries, compliance requests, or to exercise privacy rights, contact our Data Protection Office at privacy@velourcare.com.
